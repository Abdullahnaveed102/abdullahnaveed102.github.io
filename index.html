01 / Profile

Research that holds up under pressure.

I work where machine learning, reliability, and scientific computing meet.

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, advised by Prof. Guanpeng Li. My work focuses on fault tolerance and error resilience for large language models and high-performance computing applications.

I also collaborate with Argonne National Laboratory on transformer-based lossy compression for Advanced Photon Source X-ray data, working toward high throughput without compromising scientific integrity.
