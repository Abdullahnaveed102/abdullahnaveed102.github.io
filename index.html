About me

Researching reliable systems for modern AI.

I’m Abdullah, a Ph.D. student at the University of Florida. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning systems, fault tolerance, high-performance computing, and scientific data compression.

I study how large language models and HPC applications behave when hardware or software faults occur, then design lightweight mechanisms that make those systems more dependable.

I also collaborate with Argonne National Laboratory on transformer-based lossy compression for Advanced Photon Source X-ray data, working with Dr. Sheng Di and Prof. Guanpeng Li.
