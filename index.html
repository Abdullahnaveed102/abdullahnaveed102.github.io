About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, advised by Prof. Guanpeng Li in the Dependable Systems Lab. My research lies at the intersection of dependable machine learning, high-performance computing, and scientific data systems.

I study how large language models and HPC applications behave when hardware or software faults occur, and I design lightweight mechanisms that make those systems more reliable. I also collaborate with Argonne National Laboratory on transformer-based lossy compression for Advanced Photon Source X-ray data.

My current interests include:

  • Dependable AI: error resilience and fault tolerance for large language models
  • Scientific computing: learned compression for large experimental datasets
  • HPC resilience: compiler and runtime techniques for detecting transient faults
